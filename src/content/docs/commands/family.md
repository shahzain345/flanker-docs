Commands are shown without a prefix. Use your server's prefix (default `,`) or mention flanker. `(round)` parameters are required, `[square]` are optional.

## family

Marry, adopt and grow a family tree with people from any server with flanker. Your family follows you everywhere. See it at flanker.wtf/family.

**Permission:** Anyone

- **`family tree [member]`**  
  Draws the family tree. Also `tree` and `familytree`.
  example: `family tree`
- **`family marry (member)`**  
  Proposes. They get Accept and Decline buttons. Also `marry` and `propose`.; marry @someone
- **`family divorce`**  
  Ends your marriage. Also `divorce`.
- **`family adopt (member)`**  
  Asks to adopt them. Also `adopt`.; adopt @someone
- **`family makeparent (member)`**  
  Asks them to become your parent. Also `makeparent`.
- **`family disown (child)`**  
  Removes one of your children. Also `disown`.
- **`family emancipate`**  
  Leaves your parent's family. Also `emancipate` and `runaway`.
- **`family partner [member]`**  
  Shows who someone is married to. Also `partner`.
- **`family parent [member]`**  
  Shows someone's parent. Also `parent`.
- **`family children [member]`**  
  Lists someone's children (up to 15). Also `children`.
- **`family siblings [member]`**  
  Lists someone's siblings. Also `siblings`.
- **`family relationship (member) [member]`**  
  How two people are related. Also `relationship`.; relationship @a @b
- **`family size [member]`**  
  How many people are in someone's family. Also `familysize`.
- **`family block (member)`**  
  Stops someone from sending you proposals.
- **`family unblock (member)`**  
  Lets them send proposals again.

## roleplay

Anime reactions with someone (or on your own), with a running count between the two of you. Every action works on its own, like `hug @someone` or `cry`.

**Permission:** Anyone

- **`roleplay (action) [member]`**  
  airkiss, angrystare, bite, bleh, blush, brofist, celebrate, cheers, clap, confused, cool, cry, cuddle, dance, drool, evillaugh, facepalm, handhold, happy, headbang, hug, kiss, laugh, lick, mad, nervous, nom, nuzzle, nyah, pat, peek, poke, pout, punch, sad, scared, shout, shrug, shy, sigh, sip, slap, sleep, slowclap, smack, smile, smug, sneeze, sorry, stare, surprised, sweat, thumbsup, tickle, tired, wave, wink, woah, yawn, yay, yes, angry, baka, bored, feed, handshake, highfive, lurk, nod, nope, peck, shoot, think, yeet.; hug @someone
