Commands are shown without a prefix. Use your server's prefix (default `,`) or mention flanker. `(round)` parameters are required, `[square]` are optional.

## 8ball

Ask the magic 8 ball.

**Permission:** Anyone

- **`8ball (question)`**  
  Gets an answer.
  example: `8ball will it rain tomorrow`

## coinflip

Flips a coin.

**Permission:** Anyone

- **`coinflip`**  
  Heads or tails.

## roll

Rolls dice.

**Permission:** Anyone

- **`roll [sides or NdS]`**  
  Rolls a die (6 sides by default) or several, like 2d6.
  example: `roll 2d20`

## choose

Picks one of your options.

**Permission:** Anyone

- **`choose (option), (option), ...`**  
  Picks one at random.
  example: `choose pizza, sushi, tacos`

## ship

Checks the match between two people.

**Permission:** Anyone

- **`ship (name or member) [name or member]`**  
  Ships two people: their couple name, a random percentage and a heart that fills to match. With one person, pairs them with you.
  example: `ship comrade shahzain`

## urban

Looks a word up on Urban Dictionary.

**Permission:** Anyone

- **`urban (word)`**  
  The top definition and an example.

## weather

Current weather for a place.

**Permission:** Anyone

- **`weather (city)`**  
  Temperature, feels like, humidity and wind.
  example: `weather London`

## roblox

Looks up a Roblox profile.

**Permission:** Anyone

- **`roblox (username)`**  
  Display name, bio, friends, followers and join date.

## tiktok

Looks up a TikTok profile.

**Permission:** Anyone

- **`tiktok (username)`**  
  Followers, likes and videos. Best effort: TikTok sometimes blocks lookups.

## say

Makes flanker say something. Embed scripts work.

**Permission:** Manage Messages

- **`say [channel] (message)`**  
  Posts the message as flanker. Pings need Mention Everyone.
  *Manage Messages*  ·  example: `say #announcements Event starts in 10 minutes!`
