---
title: Birthdays
description: Let members set a birthday and have flanker celebrate it.
---

Members can tell flanker their birthday, and it celebrates on the day.

## For members

- Set a birthday with `,birthday` (see the [Personal commands](/commands/personal/)).
- Members can remove their birthday at any time.

## For admins

- Choose the channel where birthday messages are posted.
- Optionally assign a birthday role for the day.

## On the dashboard

Open the [dashboard](https://flanker.wtf/dashboard), pick your server, and go to
**Birthdays** to set the announcement channel, the message, and an optional
birthday role.
