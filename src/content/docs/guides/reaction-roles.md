title: Reaction roles & starboard
description: Self-assign roles with reactions or buttons, and highlight the best messages.
---

flanker's **Reactions** features let members give themselves roles, trigger
responses, and highlight popular messages on a starboard.

## Reaction roles

Let members react to a message (or click a button) to get a role, and remove
the reaction to lose it.

- Set them up with `,reaction` (see the [reaction commands](/commands/logging-invites-levels/)
  or the relevant reference page).
- You can also build button-based role pickers with the
  [card scripting](/resources/scripting/) role buttons (`role:`, `give:`, `take:`).

## Reaction triggers

Make flanker react to messages that match a word or phrase automatically.

## Starboard

A starboard reposts messages that get enough of a chosen reaction (for example
a star) into a highlights channel.

- Configure the emoji and the threshold with `,starboard`.
- Choose whether authors can star their own messages.

## On the dashboard

Open the [dashboard](https://flanker.wtf/dashboard), pick your server, and go to
**Reactions**. From there you can set up reaction roles, reaction triggers, the
starboard emoji and threshold, and reaction stats, all in one place.
