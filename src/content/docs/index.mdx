## What flanker does

<CardGrid>
  <Card title="Stops nukes" icon="shield">
    Antinuke watches for mass bans and deletions, dangerous permission grants,
    webhook and emoji spam and rogue bots, punishes whoever trips a limit, and
    undoes the damage.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Stops raids" icon="seti:lock">
    Antiraid reacts in real time with lockdown, captcha verification and
    removal of raiders before they touch your server.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Full moderation" icon="approve-check">
    Ban, timeout, jail, purge and warnings, all logged with a searchable case
    history and staff notes.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Everything else" icon="puzzle">
    Tickets, applications, welcome messages, logging, vanity roles,
    VoiceMaster, leveling, giveaways, music, a starboard and more.
  </Card>
</CardGrid>
